About Us
Be the commander of your very own robot army!
In Battlecode, your team of virtual robots will roam the screen, manage resources, and execute different offensive strategies against other teams. We were founded in 2001 and are MIT's longest running programming competition!
Plan, code, and compete throughout the month of January for a chance at more than $40,000 in prizes!

Who can participate?
Anyone is welcome to participate in Battlecode! Any team is eligible to participate in the Sprint Tournaments for prize money. However, only student teams (including non-MIT student teams) are eligible for the remaining tournaments and prize money.
