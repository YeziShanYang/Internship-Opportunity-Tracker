Discovery Programme
The Discovery Programme is an opportunity open to students that are two years away from graduation. The programme offers students a unique opportunity to gain exposure to our business, develop essential professional skills and connect with Barclays employees.

What is Discovery?
Part 1
You’ll learn all about Barclays: who we are and what we do. You’ll also enjoy interacting with us through technical, soft skills and networking workshops.
Part 2
You will have the opportunity to be introduced to the next steps in order to kick start your career at Barclays. The programme may vary depending on the location.
The Highlights
Discover your path
Dive into a world of possibilities&mdash; from technology and operations to finance and investment banking&mdash;and find the path that inspires you.
Do and Develop
Get a behind-the-scenes look at a global company and start building your understanding of the professional world.
Get a Head-start
Participants of our Discovery Programme may be considered for a 2027 internship.
