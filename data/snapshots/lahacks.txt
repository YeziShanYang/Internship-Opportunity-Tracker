This is LA Hacks.
A free, student-run hackathon at UCLA where 1,000+ builders turn ideas into real projects in just 36 hours. Find your team, learn through workshops, and build alongside mentors and industry experts—whatever your experience level.
Now entering its 15th year, LA Hacks has grown into one of Southern California&#x27;s largest collegiate hackathons, bringing students together to create what comes next.
