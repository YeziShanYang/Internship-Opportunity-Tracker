Insight Programs (EMEA only)
Nomura has two Spring Internship Insight Programs designed to help 1st
year students on a 3 year degree course (and 2nd year students on a 4 year
degree course) explore the world of investment banking and experience the
variety of career paths available. The program runs in April and you will
have the opportunity to gain exposure across all our business functions,
helping you to decide if a career in banking is right for you and to identify
where your skill set is best suited within our business.
We will give you the opportunity to
put your academic knowledge into
practice through interactive case
studies, workshops and desk
shadowing.
Nomura is passionate about
developing talent from an early stage
and our Programs act as the talent
pipeline for our summer internships
and ultimately, our Graduate
Programs.
Applications for our Insight Programs open from 1st of September. We
recruit on a rolling basis and therefore advise you to apply as soon as
possible, and in advance of the application deadline. We reserve the right to
close a vacancy ahead of the initially stated deadline if all spots on the
program are filled.

Women's Immersion Program (Two Weeks — Unpaid)
Program Overview
The Women’s Immersion Program offers a two week unpaid work experience in April in one of our front office divisions:
Investment Banking (IBD)
Global Markets (GM)
You will have the chance to gain an in-depth insight into Nomura and the world of Investment Banking through:
Case study exercises
Graduate panel discussions
Business overview presentations
Technical and soft skills training
Networking and social events
Buddy support network
Training
Your insight training is divided into technical work and soft skills development. You’ll have the chance to start expanding your financial knowledge with classroom based learning ahead of joining a team on the GM or IB floor:
Training on Financial Markets, Accounting, Excel and Trading
On the desk technical training
Case study exercises
Interview skills and CV workshops
Presentation skills training
Assessment
During your time with Nomura, you will work on an individual project, which will be presented as part of your assessment.
Pending performance you will have the opportunity to be fast tracked for a 2026 summer internship program interviews.
SEO Explore Nomura (Four Days — Unpaid)
Program Overview
The SEO Explore Nomura Program offers 4-day classroom based insights across our Investment Banking, Global Markets and Corporate Infrastructure divisions. The program runs in April in London and is hosted in partnership with our charity partner in SEO London (Sponsors for Educational Opportunity) and therefore the program is exclusively for affiliated members of SEO. The vacancy is not advertised on the Nomura website as SEO holds the application link to this vacancy and will be sharing it directly with its members.
Within this program, you will have the opportunity to gain an insight into Nomura and the world of Investment Banking, Global Markets and Technology through:
Case study exercises
Graduate panel discussions
Business overview presentations
Mock Interviews
Group Projects
Networking and social events
Buddy support network
Training
You will receive the following training as part of the Explore program:
An introduction to Banking & Finance
Soft skills development workshops
Interview Preparation, including case study workshops.
Assessment
Pending performance, after your 4 days with Nomura, you will have the opportunity to be fast tracked for a 2026 summer internship program interviews.
Applications for our Insight Programs open from 1st September. We recruit on a rolling basis and therefore
advise you to apply as soon as possible, and in advance of the application deadline specified on the vacancy.
For more information on the business divisions refer to What We Do .
