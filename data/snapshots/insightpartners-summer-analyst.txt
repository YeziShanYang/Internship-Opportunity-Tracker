About the Summer Investment Analyst Program
As a sourcing analyst on Insight's Investment Team, you are the lifeblood of our firm. Without you, our deal pipeline does not exist. Hustle, intellectual curiosity and a passion for technology are integral to our culture and role.
As an analyst, you will:
&bull;Learn how to effectively speak with entrepreneurs to evaluate whether their business is worth considering for investment
&bull;Understand different business models that are attractive to Insight
&bull;Gain insight into how deals are structured and modeled
&bull;Get exposure into all aspects of the lifecycle of a deal
&bull;Be supported by an analyst mentor, your team members, and partners
The ideal candidate is excited to find great companies, eager to get involved in deals and develop investment memos, and creative when it comes to sourcing M&amp;A targets for our portfolio companies and prospective investments. If you are a team player, possess an outgoing personality and enjoy meeting new people, then this is the perfect role for you!
About the Inclusion by Insight Summit
Dates: January 14th &amp; 15th, 2027
Insight is committed to broadening access to the tech investing ecosystem. We strive to build an organization with a wide range of experiences and backgrounds, reflecting our standards of integrity and excellence. Our two-day Inclusion by Insight Virtual Summit is an integral part of our recruiting efforts.
Over the course of the two days, attendees will have an opportunity to explore the Venture Capital, Private Equity, and tech investing space, become familiar with Insight&rsquo;s business and culture, learn what it takes to be successful throughout the recruiting season, and network with Insight professionals.
After the summit, select attendees will be invited to participate in a first round superday to join the 2028 Investment Summer Analyst Program.

Meet a Previous Investment Summer Analyst
Sahar Mohammadzadeh
Associate
Hometown: Lexington, Kentucky
Joined Insight: 2021
Sahar began her journey at Insight as an Investment Summer Analyst and has since grown her career within the firm - progressing from Summer Analyst to Full-Time Analyst and now Associate.
From day one, Insight&rsquo;s Summer Analysts are trusted with real responsibility, including opportunities to engage directly with founders and develop critical investing skills like pattern recognition. That early exposure and autonomy make the summer program a truly&nbsp;worthwhile experience for college students ea-ger to jump-start their careers.
Since returning full-time, Sahar has continued to build on the strong foundation she developed during her internship, taking on new challenges and expanding her skill set along the way.
For students considering the summer program, Sahar encourages making the most of the experience: She&nbsp;believes that one of the best parts of Insight is the opportunity to learn from so many talented inves-tors, each with their own style and approach - whether it&rsquo;s investing strategies, storytelling techniques, or boardroom dynamics. Insight&rsquo;s approachable culture makes it easy to build real connections, so take advantage of your summer to gain exposure to as many perspectives and skill sets as you can.
At Insight, the learning never stops - and Sahar&rsquo;s journey is a great example of the growth and opportunity that the program offers.
Alexandra Lundin
Vice President
Alexandra joined Insight as an Investment Summer Analyst and has since grown into her current role as Vice President. She describes her path at Insight as "organic," shaped by the natural evolution of her role alongside the firm&rsquo;s broader growth over time. At every stage, Alexandra highlights that the core of the Insight experience remains consistent: a focus on autonomy, creativity, and a strong drive for con-tinuous learning.
When asked what&rsquo;s kept her at Insight, Alexandra reflects that at its heart, her role is about learning - and for her, there&rsquo;s nothing more rewarding. Insight offers the freedom and flexibility to explore your interests and shape the kind of investor you want to become, all supported by a deep bench of re-sources and expertise built over 30 years.
Beyond the work itself, Alexandra emphasizes that it&rsquo;s the people who make Insight truly special. The culture fosters real friendships built on mutual respect and collaboration, and feels far more horizontal and fluid than traditional firm hierarchies. She credits much of her growth to what she&rsquo;s learned not just from mentors, but also from peers and the teams she manages - a true reflection of Insight&rsquo;s inclusive, supportive environment.

Summer Analyst FAQs
What does an application consist of?
A completed application entails submitting a copy of your resume, transcript, and completing a HireVue Video Cover Letter.
When will my application be reviewed?
We review applications on a rolling basis in the order in which they were completed, so it is advantageous to submit your application materials as soon as possible. We will be kicking off our superdays in January and will look to host 2 superdays a month thereafter until we fill the class.
How can I learn more about the Summer Program?
We encourage you to review our Campus Recruiting Virtual Series for a deeper dive into the analyst experience!
Can I apply to both the Summer Program and the Inclusion by Insight Summit?
Absolutely! We encourage applicants to apply to both programs as we review applications in order of when they were completed. The earlier you get in both applications, the better!
