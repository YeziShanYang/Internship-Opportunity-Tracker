Senior Blockchain Engineer
Solayer Labs Inc. is hiring a full-time Senior Blockchain Engineer.
Solayer Labs Inc. is hiring a full-time Senior Blockchain Engineer.
POSITION DETAILS
Job Duties:
Design, develop, and maintain blockchain systems and distributed applications to support secure and reliable transaction processing.
Develop algorithms using mathematical and statistical methods, and implement them in software to improve blockchain system performance.
Design and implement blockchain components, including consensus mechanisms and smart contracts, to improve system security and performance.
Analyze system performance, identify issues, and improve algorithms and distributed systems for greater efficiency and reliability.
Lead the design and development of blockchain systems by providing technical guidance, reviewing code and system designs, troubleshooting technical issues, and evaluating new blockchain technologies.
Telecommuting is an option.
Job Requirements:
POSITION TYPE / FULL-TIME PERMANENT
EDUCATION REQUIRED / MASTER’S DEGREE
Master’s degree in Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, or a closely related field. Must have knowledge of probability and numerical analysis.
Rate of pay: $121,014 - $200,000 per year
