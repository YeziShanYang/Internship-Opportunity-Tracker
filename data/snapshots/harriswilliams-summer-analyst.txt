Unlock Your Potential This Summer
The Summer Analyst Program is a 10-week, full-immersion experience designed to give students a deep dive into investment banking. As a Summer Analyst at Harris Williams, you’ll contribute from the start, working alongside senior professionals, taking on meaningful responsibilities, and supporting live client engagements. This program is built for students who want to learn by doing and leave the summer with a strong foundation for a world-class career.
Program duration: 10 weeks
Locations: Boston, London, Minneapolis, New York, and San Francisco
Timing: Summer 2028
Application window: October 2026 – January 2027

Application Details
Who should apply?
United States: Students who will be rising seniors at the time of the internship.
London Office: Penultimate-year students pursuing a BSc or MSc.
Application timeline
Application and interview timing varies by region.
United States
Applications typically open during the late fall semester of your sophomore year.
Interviews typically take place during the early spring semester of your sophomore year.
London Office
Applications typically open in late August or early September.
Applications generally remain open for three to four weeks, though early application is encouraged as candidates are reviewed on a rolling basis.
Interviews take place during the autumn recruiting season, with final-round interviews typically concluding by the end of October.
