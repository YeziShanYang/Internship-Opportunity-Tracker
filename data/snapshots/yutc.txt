The Competition
YUTC is two days of trading scenarios and games designed by YUTC organizers and sponsors. Between rounds, competitors network with peers and recruiters from top trading firms. Travel to and from New Haven is reimbursed, and competitors from outside Yale have hotel accommodations arranged for the weekend. The competition is open to undergraduate and graduate students worldwide, and no trading experience is necessary.
